# vision.md — Product Vision

> Source of truth for product direction. All CRs must align with this.

## Product Name
Creative Brief Agent

## One-Line Description
An AI-driven conversational agent that guides clients and creative teams through natural dialogue to produce a complete, structured, and actionable creative brief.

## Problem Being Solved
Briefing processes are slow, inconsistent, and depend entirely on the client's ability to articulate their own needs. Static forms miss nuance. Ambiguities discovered late in production cause expensive rework. There is no tool that behaves like a skilled strategic partner during the briefing process itself.

## Target Users
- Creative agencies running client briefing sessions
- In-house creative teams receiving briefs from internal stakeholders
- Clients who need to commission creative work (campaigns, branding, digital products)
- Creative directors and strategists facilitating briefing workshops

## Core Value Proposition
Feels like a senior strategic advisor conducting a bespoke workshop — not a form to fill out. Surfaces contradictions, closes information gaps, challenges weak strategic choices, and produces a brief that is genuinely actionable. Reduces briefing time while increasing output quality.

## Product Principles
1. Conversation first — never feels like a form
2. Living brief state — structured data built in real time from dialogue
3. Reasoning AI — detects gaps, contradictions, and weak positions
4. Transparency — user sees the brief being built as the conversation proceeds
5. Non-autonomous — AI is controlled and deterministic; never takes irreversible actions

## Out of Scope (v1 / MVP)
- Multi-agent autonomy
- CRM integrations
- Project management features
- Team collaboration / multi-user sessions
- Billing and subscription management
- Notion export (post-MVP)
- Complex automated downstream workflows

## Success Metrics
- Time to complete a brief: target < 30 minutes
- Brief completeness score: all required sections filled, no unresolved contradictions
- User-reported confidence in the brief at export
- % of generated briefs exported and used in downstream creative work
